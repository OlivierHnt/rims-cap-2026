RIMS Workshop September 9–11, 2026 • Kyoto, Japan ⛩️

Development of Computer-Assisted Proofs in Dynamical Systems

fig. 1 — Shimizu–Morioka attractor

About

This workshop brings together researchers working on rigorous, computer-assisted methods for the study of dynamical systems. Topics span validated numerics, and constructive proofs in finite- and infinite-dimensional dynamical systems.
